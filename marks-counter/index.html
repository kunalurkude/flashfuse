A marks counter that totals a stack of exam papers in minutes

Tally Pro is a free app for adding up marks. Type each answer sheet's marks on a big keypad and it keeps a running grand total and paper count, so you never have to re-add a column on a calculator again.

Start counting marks

Why teachers use it

Checking answer sheets is slow enough. Adding them up on a normal calculator is where mistakes creep in: a missed key, a double entry, or losing your place halfway down the pile. Tally Pro is built for that one job.

  • Auto-add. Type a paper's marks and stop. After a short pause (0.5, 1, 2 or 3 seconds, or your own delay) it adds the mark by itself, so you can keep your eyes on the papers.
  • Live grand total and paper count stay on screen the whole time.
  • Fix mistakes in place. Tap any entry to edit it, or delete it, without starting over.
  • Half marks. The keypad has a decimal point for marks like 7.5.
  • Made for thumbs. Large keys with a small vibration on every tap. On a laptop or tablet, your keyboard works too.

How to total exam marks with Tally Pro

  1. Open Tally Pro on your phone.
  2. Pick an auto-add delay, or tap the add button yourself after each paper.
  3. Type the marks from the first answer sheet. The paper count and grand total update.
  4. Move to the next sheet and repeat until the pile is done.
  5. Read off the total and the number of papers. Divide one by the other for the class average.

Install it like an app, use it offline

Tally Pro is a Progressive Web App, so there's no app store download. Open it once, then:

  • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share → "Add to Home Screen".

It gets its own icon, opens full-screen and keeps working with no internet: in a staff room, an exam hall or on the bus home.

Private by design

There's no sign-up and no server. The marks you type stay on your phone and are never uploaded anywhere.

Common questions

Is Tally Pro free?

Yes. No ads, no trial and no account.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first visit and adding it to your home screen, it works fully offline.

Can I enter half marks?

Yes. Use the decimal point key, for example 7.5.

Does it save my marks to a server?

No. Nothing you type leaves your device.
