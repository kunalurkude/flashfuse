For teachers · PWA

Tally Pro

Total a stack of exam papers in minutes. Type each paper's marks on a big keypad and Tally Pro adds them up as you go.

  • Auto-add. Stop typing and the mark adds itself after 0.5s, 1s, 2s, 3s or your own delay.
  • Live grand total and paper count, always on screen.
  • Fix mistakes fast. Edit or delete any paper's entry.
  • Made for thumbs. Large keys with a tiny vibration on every tap.

For EV owners & builders · Free

EV Studio

Know your electric vehicle's real range and running cost, then design the battery pack to match.

  • Range, cost & charge time. Range band, cost per km and per month, and charge time from your charger's current.
  • Real-world numbers. Consumption bands from published Indian EV tests, charging losses and petrol comparison included.
  • Battery pack builder. Real cell datasheets (Samsung, EVE): series/parallel layout, voltage window, current limits and weight.
  • Show the math. Tap any result to see the formula with your numbers in it.
Open EV Studio Phone · Tablet · Desktop
For engineers & fleets · Pro edition

EV Studio Pro

The advanced battery performance suite, live at evrex.in. Everything in EV Studio, plus real-world conditions and a full cell library.

  • Real-world range. Temperature, driving style, terrain and usable charge window change the result.
  • 30+ real cells including Tesla 4680, BYD Blade, CATL, Samsung SDI, LG Chem and sodium-ion.
  • Weight and volume estimates for the finished pack, with busbar overhead.
  • Live cell matrix. A schematic of your series and parallel groups as you build.

Questions

Something else? Ask us directly.

Are the apps really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no paid tier and no ads. We make money building custom apps for businesses, not from you.

Where is my data stored?

Nowhere but your device. The apps don't send what you type to any server, and there are no accounts, ads or trackers.

How do I install an app on my phone?

Open the app, then use your browser menu. On Android Chrome tap ⋮ → "Install app". On iPhone Safari tap Share → "Add to Home Screen".

Do they work without internet?

Tally Pro installs as an app and keeps working offline. The EV tools need a connection to load, then every calculation runs on your device.

Can you build an app like this for my business?

Yes. Tell us what you want to calculate, track or configure and we'll reply with an idea, a timeline and a quote.
